Grey · Identity System · brand-level

Identity System

What is the story of this artifact, and what is the most natural way for that story to exist in the world?

Brand-level system for Grey / Grey Objects. Sourced from Style Reference v0.1, Design System HTML, and the communications research digest. Collection-specific launches wait on locked concept.

§ 01

Core idea

“Grey is not defined by what it sells. It is defined by what Grey notices, collects, transforms, and chooses to give meaning to. The store is the entry point; the gallery is the world.”

A white-cube gallery that happens to take payment. Grey notices, collects, transforms, and gives meaning. The interface stays silent so the objects can speak.

Principles · settle arguments
1
The object speaks firstInterface, copy, and motion step back until the work is seen.
2
Rooms, not feedsContent is paged and stepped. Every view is complete and composed. No infinite scroll anywhere.
3
Label, don't sellDescribe like a curator, never like an advert. Facts, provenance, process.
4
Meaning through interventionShow what Grey did (One of None) or what Grey noticed (Selection Collection). That distinction is always visible.
5
Commerce is quiet, not hiddenPrice and Acquire are always one glance away, but they never shout.

Universe: Grey (Grey Experience). Store: Grey Objects. Site is the center of gravity; social media creates awareness and points here.

Brand architecture — locked collections: One of None; Selection Collection; Cyber Gunk; Evergrey; Objects (temporary title). Dialects TBD where not locked; inherit Grey foundation. No .

§ 03

Communication objective

Make the site where people browse, discover, read, listen, and sometimes acquire. Social creates awareness and always points back. Commerce stays quiet, never hidden.

Grammar is museum, not e-commerce. Rooms, not feeds.

§ 04

Narrative

A white-cube gallery that happens to take payment. Meaning shows through intervention or noticing. Deadpan gravity on ordinary finds is the wit. Culture is the primary sentence; checkout is a footnote.

Shown
  • Selection, material fact, transformation — what Grey did or noticed
  • Provenance as place and when — enough: “Found in Shoreditch, 2025.”
  • Status as fact — Available · By Request · Not for Sale · Collected
Withheld
  • Lifestyle filler and explanatory noise — do not annotate every object to death
  • Urgency and scarcity theatre — no “only 2 left”, countdowns, Sold Out
  • Instagram-first default — feed earns a place only with a reason
Founder presence

Founder is a creative tool, not the default. Use presence when it clarifies meaning (criteria of noticing, like a gallery director). Default to silence so the object remains the hero. Borrow the register of quiet object-first peers (Aesop / The Row silence); refuse founder-as-celebrity engine.

§ 06

Visual identity summary

Full interactive specs live in the Design System HTML companion and Style Reference (DESIGN.md). Summary for communications:

  • Colour — Wall #ECECE9, Plinth #F7F7F5, Ink #111110, Graphite, Grey, Fog; Red Dot #D7261E as rule, 8px dot, or focus only. Objects bring colour; interface never does.
  • Type — Host Grotesk (building / UI / headings / body / display, 400–500), Newsreader Italic (work titles only), IBM Plex Mono (record). Eurostile locked for the wordmark only (custom-kerned SVGs are SoT). Never Eurostile for body, UI, or headlines.
  • Shape — 0px radius everywhere; only the status dot is round. No shadows.
  • Motion — doors and lights; slow; ease-door / ease-light; never springy.
Logo

From Style Reference · Visual · Grey foundation · Logo. Eurostile locked as the logo face (custom-kerned cut). Vector source of truth: brand/grey-wordmark-naked.svg + brand/grey-wordmark-oval.svg (PNG sheet kept as raster reference). Host Grotesk remains the building face for UI, navigation, headings, body, and display.

Visual usage guide below — tiles render from SVG with live fill colour per ground.

  • Oval seal — Naked wordmark is the default on everyday surfaces (nav, letterhead, UI). Oval reserved for the seal: collection covers, launch moments, rare stamped moments. At most one oval per view.
  • Placement / inversion — Logo tone is always the direct inverse of its ground. On #FFFFFF → #000000; on #CDCDCD → #383838; on #383838 → #CDCDCD; on #000000 → #FFFFFF. No midtones. No fixed colour.
  • Never — outline, shadow, stretch, casual pill/badge, animate letterforms, red mark, centred in nav.
  • Clear space / min — Cap-height of the G on all sides; 14px cap height minimum on screen; top-left corner mark.

§ 08

Content system

Rules that hold without a person in the room — encode the idea into caption order, status language, and what never appears.

  • Always — object first; fixed label order; Acquire only; rooms not feeds; point social to the site
  • Never appears — Buy/cart urgency; Sold Out; emoji; exclamation; scarcity countdowns; lifestyle props/models; founder-as-feed default; infinite scroll copy
  • Open vessels — marks and pages accrue meaning from objects over time; do not over-furnish the story on day one
§ 09

Do's and don'ts

Do
  • Fit every view in the viewport. Step between rooms.
  • Red only as a rule, a dot, or a focus ring. One red element per view at rest.
  • Work titles in Newsreader Italic, nothing else.
  • Every number in IBM Plex Mono, tabular.
  • 0px radius on everything but the status dot.
  • Labels in the fixed anatomy order, left-aligned.
  • Acquire as the only purchase verb.
Don't
  • Infinite scroll, parallax, “load more”.
  • Drop shadows, blur, glassmorphism, UI gradients.
  • Rounded corners or pill buttons.
  • Font weight above 500.
  • Red as fill, background, or text blocks.
  • Urgency language, emoji, exclamation marks, icon libraries.
  • More than one filled button in a view.
  • Autoplaying sound.
§ 10

Asset requirements

  • Photography — objects as installed works; wall-grey or real wall; soft even light; no props or hands. 4:5 tiles; full-bleed plates. Selection Collection: shoot as found.
  • Optional — monochrome find-map with one red dot; per-object ambient sound off by default.
  • No — lifestyle, models, marble flat-lays, stock, AI-looking renders.
  • Logo — Eurostile locked (custom-kerned). Vector SoT: grey-wordmark-naked.svg + grey-wordmark-oval.svg (PNG sheet = raster reference). Oval = seal only; inversion greys #FFFFFF / #CDCDCD / #383838 / #000000. Host Grotesk for all non-mark type.
  • Film / certificates — Night takeover for film; certificate carries full label anatomy.

Imagery section proposed until Creative Director sign-off.

§ 11

Production brief

Write like a curator, not an advert. Sentence case. No !, no emoji, no ALL-CAPS sentences, no urgency. £ whole amounts. Dimensions with ×. Year-only on labels. Titles italic serif; numbers mono. One filled action per view: Acquire — £{price}.

Plate: what it is, what Grey did or noticed, one detail. Full record ~80 words. Point every social line back to the site.
